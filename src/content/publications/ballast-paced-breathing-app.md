After reading [*Breath* by James Nestor](/books/breath), I wanted to give box breathing a real try: inhale for four seconds, hold for four, exhale for four, hold for four, repeat. The problem was keeping the count. Counting in my head pulled my attention away from the breathing itself, and the breathing app built into my Apple Watch only paces a simple inhale and exhale. I wanted something I could start from my wrist, close my eyes, and just follow. So I built Ballast, an app that keeps the count for you. On the Apple Watch a gentle haptic tap marks every phase change, so you can keep your eyes closed and follow along without ever looking at a screen. There is also an iPhone app that guides you through each breath visually. You can [watch it in action here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYen-MDYbns). It is free, with no account and no ads, so give it a try and I hope it helps you breathe a little easier.
