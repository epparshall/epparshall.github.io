Jeans are a bad travel choice. They are heavy, they take forever to dry, and they are uncomfortable on a long flight. I pack Eddie Bauer's Rainier pants in the athletic fit instead, because one pair can handle a travel day, a full day of walking around a city, and a hike without looking out of place at any of them.

### Why I like them

- **Comfortable for long days:** The nylon-spandex fabric has two-way stretch and articulated knees, so they are comfortable on an overnight flight or a long bus ride and do not restrict you on a climb.
- **Quick drying and water resistant:** A water-repellent finish sheds light rain, and if they get wet or you wash them in a sink, they dry quickly. That makes them easy to rewear on a carry-on-only trip.
- **Built-in sun protection:** The fabric is rated UPF 50+, which is one less thing to worry about on a long day outside.
- **Zippered thigh pocket:** Your phone or passport stays put on a crowded bus or metro, which matters in places where pickpocketing is common.
- **Athletic fit:** They sit close to the body with a clean, streamlined cut, so they look normal at dinner instead of like baggy hiking gear.

### Small tradeoffs

- They are not warm. In cold weather you will want a base layer underneath.
- They look like travel pants, not dress pants, so they are not the move for a formal dinner.

### Practical takeaway

Pair these with [proper running shoes](/travel/resources/running-shoes) and you have an outfit that works for almost every travel day. One good pair of travel pants lets you pack lighter, because you are not bringing jeans for one thing and hiking pants for another.
