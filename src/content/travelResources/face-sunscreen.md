Sun on the road adds up fast. You are outside far more than you are at home, often at higher altitude or closer to the equator, and a sunburned face can ruin a few days of a trip. My fix is to make sunscreen part of the morning routine instead of something I remember halfway through the day. I use CeraVe's AM facial moisturizer with SPF 50.

**Get the right one.** CeraVe sells a few similar-looking AM moisturizers. The one I use is the lightweight SPF 50 version linked below, not the oil-free one.

### Why I use it

- **Moisturizer and sunscreen in one step:** You put it on once in the morning and you are covered. Fewer steps means you actually do it every day.
- **SPF 50, mineral based:** It uses micro-fine zinc oxide for broad-spectrum UVA and UVB protection.
- **Lightweight and non-greasy:** It absorbs quickly and does not leave a white cast, so it does not feel like you are wearing beach sunscreen on your face.
- **Carry-on friendly:** The bottle is 3 ounces, under the 3.4 ounce TSA limit, so it goes in your liquids bag without a second thought.
- **HSA eligible (Americans only):** Broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 15 or higher counts as an HSA and FSA expense, so you can pay for it with pre-tax money.

### Small tradeoffs

- It is not water resistant. If you are swimming or sweating, this is not going to cut it. Use a water-resistant sunscreen like a [clip-on SPF 50](/travel/resources/clip-on-sunscreen/) for those days.
- It is a face product, so you still need regular sunscreen for your arms, neck, and legs on beach or hiking days.
- Like any sunscreen, it wears off. Reapply every couple of hours if you are out in strong sun.

### Practical takeaway

UV gets stronger the higher you climb, which I noticed on the volcano hike in [Guatemala](/travel/guatemala), and you are outside most of the day when you travel. A daily SPF moisturizer is the easiest way to protect your face without having to think about it.
