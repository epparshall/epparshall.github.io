Sunscreen only works where you put it, and the spots people miss are the ones a hat covers: your scalp, the tops of your ears, and the back of your neck. A good sun hat also never wears off and never needs reapplying. This one is my pick for any trip with long days outside.

### Why I like it

- **UPF 50+ fabric:** The material is rated UPF 50+, which blocks about 98% of UV rays.
- **Wide brim plus a neck flap:** The brim shades your face and ears, and the long flap in the back covers your neck. When you do not need the flap, it tucks away and the hat works as a regular bucket hat.
- **Stays cool:** Mesh vents let air through the crown, and a moisture-wicking sweatband helps on hot, humid days.
- **Stays on in the wind:** When it gets windy, bring the chin string into play and cinch it, and the hat stays on your head instead of flying off a summit or a boat. When it is calm, the string stays out of the way.
- **Hooks onto your backpack:** When you head inside, it hooks easily onto a backpack, so you are not stuck holding it or stuffing it into your bag.
- **Light and quick drying:** It is a light polyester and nylon blend, so it dries fast if it gets wet or sweaty.

### Small tradeoffs

- It looks like a fishing hat, because it basically is one. It is built for function, not style.
- The care label says hand wash only.

### Practical takeaway

A hat covers the spots sunscreen misses and never needs reapplying. Pair it with a [clip-on sunscreen](/travel/resources/clip-on-sunscreen/) for everything else, and with [UPF 50+ travel pants](/travel/resources/travel-pants/) if you will be outside all day.
