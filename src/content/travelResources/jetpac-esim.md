### Important limitation (this matters)

Jetpac is **data-only**, not a full phone plan. This means:
- **No regular phone number service:** You won't have a local or US number for traditional calls.
- **No SMS texts:** Standard green bubble texts won't work.
- **iMessage/FaceTime still work:** Since these use data, they work normally, though they will show up from your Apple ID/email rather than your phone number.
- **WhatsApp, Signal, etc. work normally:** These apps are data-based and are unaffected.

### Practical takeaway

Jetpac is best when you want cheap, simple travel data without the hassle of SIM swapping or hunting for local shops. It’s not a replacement for a full cellular plan, but for travel, it removes a lot of friction as long as you understand the data-only trade-off.
